Got questions?

Frequently asked questions

What broker does OptionLensa work with? +
OptionLensa works exclusively with Interactive Brokers (IBKR). You need TWS (Trader Workstation) or IB Gateway running on your computer with API connections enabled on port 7497 (paper) or 7496 (live).
Does OptionLensa work on Mac or Linux? +
Currently OptionLensa is a Windows-only application (.exe). Mac and Linux support may be added in a future version.
Do I need a live IBKR account? +
No — OptionLensa works with both paper trading and live accounts. You can test everything with a paper trading account first.
What is a conditional order? +
A conditional order is triggered automatically when the underlying stock hits a price you set. For example: sell your NVDA $195 PUT when NVDA stock drops below $193. No need to watch the screen.
How does the license work? +
Each license is tied to one device. Your license is verified against your active Whop subscription. If your subscription lapses, the tool will prompt you to renew.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes — monthly subscribers can cancel at any time from their Whop account. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.
Is my data safe? +
OptionLensa connects locally to your TWS installation. No trade data, account information or positions are sent to any external server.

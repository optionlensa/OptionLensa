## 📥 Download & Install

1. Purchase a license at **https://whop.com/option-lensa**
2. You will receive a **license key** after purchase — check Whop → User → Memberships
3. Download **OptionLensa.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/optionlensa/OptionLensa/releases/latest)
4. Make sure TWS or IB Gateway is open and connected
5. Double-click `OptionLensa.exe`
6. **Windows defender warning** might pop up. Click **More info > Run anyway**
7. Open your browser at `http://localhost:5050`
8. Enter your license key when prompted

## ⚡ What is OptionLensa?

OptionLensa is a local desktop tool that connects directly to your Interactive Brokers TWS or IB Gateway via the TWS API. It scans your watchlist for the best options contracts in real time — filtered by delta, IV, spread, and volume — so you can focus on trading, not searching.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Live options chain scanner** — filtered by delta, IV, spread & volume
- **Most Volume, OTM & ITM** ranked in order
- **One-click entry** with risk-based position sizing
- **TWS chart sync** — one click pushes the contract to your linked TWS chart
- **Conditional orders** — TP/SL triggered by stock price movements
- **OCO bracket orders** — take profit and stop loss linked together
- **Live portfolio panel** — real-time unrealized P&L
- **Pending orders tab** — view and cancel all active IBKR orders
- **Multi-ticker watchlist** with live prices

---

## 💻 Requirements

- Interactive Brokers account (live or paper)
- TWS or IB Gateway running on port **7497** (paper) or **7496** (live)
- Windows 10 or later
